## Target
Keep the deliberately rough, charming 45-second notebook-sketch look, but add **just enough detail for instant recognition**. Never simplify away identifying attributes.

- Use fine, slightly wobbly **dark blue ballpoint-pen strokes** (#24528C is a starting reference) on a **pure white** background.
- Maintain natural irregularity, small asymmetries, no fills except tiny eyes, no shading, no gradients and no polished vector styling.
- Guiem: oversized uneven circular head; dot eyes, tiny curved smile, two or three short hair strokes; one-line torso and limbs.
- **Glasses, when present in the source:** show two unmistakable thin oval or rounded frames encircling the eyes, joined by a small bridge and short temple arms. Do not replace glasses with just dots or a line.
- Preserve other recognizable silhouette-level details when present: beard/stubble, long hair, graduation cap, EEG cap, yoga pose, wheelchair, train, signed document, airplane, heart, security shield, etc. Use at most 2–5 extra strokes for a distinctive attribute.
- Keep each milestone's original story and props. Aim for a little **more** information than the current PR #18 images, not a return to elaborate cartoon drawings.
- Do not give every image the same pose or accessories.
- One consistent blue ink shade and line weight for all 13 JPEGs, including `guiem_cs_young.jpg`.
- Preserve the original 13 filenames, square canvas, and repository paths. Preview as a contact sheet.
- Keep the pull request unmerged.

**Success criterion:** A friendly stick-figure drawing that still reads as a person wearing glasses when the original person wore glasses, and whose career milestone is identifiable at a glance.
